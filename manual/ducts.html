Ducts and duct sizing

Contents

Read the duct sizing

What it is for. See the duct sizes, airflow, velocity and pressure loss for each duct section.

Where. The Duct sizing page, the Duct sizing card. Both editions.

What you get.

  • At the top: Available static, Longest supply TEL and Longest return TEL (total effective length), Friction rate and Worst-case static.
  • Notes for rooms that need more registers, for example "Primary bed: 221 CFM per register. Consider adding a register."
  • The Duct sections table: type, role, material, cfm, length, fitting equivalent length, round and rectangular size, velocity (fpm), friction per 100 ft, duct loss, fitting loss, pressure drop (in. w.c.) and notes.
  • Each section's Material list can be changed on the row (sheet metal, fiberglass duct board, flex fully stretched, flex at 4% or 15% compression).

Change the inputs. Press Duct design inputs. It opens the Design conditions page, where the friction rate, static, duct heights, materials, joints and takeoffs are set.

Limits. The designer of record checks the sizes, the static and the velocities.

Shop outputs

Where. The Duct sizing page. Both editions.

  • Piece marks: marks for each piece from the fabrication isos. Preview labels shows the labels; Labels (PDF) and Piece list (CSV) save them. Pick the Label stock.
  • Flat patterns and nesting: rectangular straight duct, elbows, transitions and square-to-rounds laid out flat with the allowances in the card and nested on sheet stock. Preview cut sheets, Cut sheets and nesting (PDF).
  • Hangers and supports: tick Lay out hangers and Show them on the plan; set the spacing, hanger type, upper attachment and rod.
  • Shop package and CAM cut files: Shop package (PDF), Preview, CAM cut files (DXF, ZIP), View cut files, Batch list (CSV). Group pieces by sets the batches.

Limits. The allowances and hanger spacings in these cards are settings: set them to your shop's own values.
