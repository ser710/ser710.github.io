Aerion AI

Contents

Aerion AI reads the open design and answers questions about it, runs one-click tasks, and in Agent mode can make approved changes. It is a paid add-on on top of the Aerion subscription, $10 a month. Everything here is in both editions; some tasks show only in the edition they apply to.

Get the AI add-on

What it is for. Turn Aerion AI on for your license.

Where. AI in the top bar, and Options, card Aerion AI. Both editions.

Steps.

  1. Open the AI panel (AI) and press Get the AI add-on, or press See the AI add-on on Options. Checkout opens with your license's email filled in.
  2. After paying, press Check again in the panel. Aerion fetches a new license key with the AI on it.

What you get. Questions go to Aerion's AI service through the license server, which checks the key, that the add-on is paid, and the month's allowance. No AI key is in the app. The server keeps nothing that is asked or answered, only the month's usage count.

Limits. The add-on has a monthly allowance. Without the add-on, the AI cards on Options are not there and the AI settings do nothing.

Ask a question or run a task

What it is for. Get a review, an explanation or a draft from the design as it stands.

Where. AI in the top bar. The panel says it reads your design and never changes it (in Ask mode). Both editions.

Steps.

  1. Type a question and press Ask, or press a one-click task.
  2. Clear starts over.

One-click tasks.

  • Review this design, Explain the design checks, Ask about the selection, Check equipment sizing, Draft general notes, Homeowner summary.
  • Scope of work letter (the letter in clear professional wording), Installer notes (units and where, duct sizes run by run, registers, special items), What is left to finish, Permit submittal list (what the submittal needs, what the project has, what is missing), Ways to lower the cost (three options with what each gives up), Email to the customer, Walk-through script and Maintenance plan.

What the AI sees. The loads, systems, ducts, registers and design checks, and also high wall units, room pressure, the feet of duct by insulation, outdoor units and line sets, ventilation and exhaust, thermostats, the client and company details that are filled in, what the proposal includes and excludes, and the scope of work facts. The scope letter's full text goes only with the tasks and questions that use it.

Limits. Check every number the AI gives against the design. An empty reply says why (the AI declined, or ran out of room). The panel's footer says how much smaller the data was sent (the token saver writes lists as tables and lets the AI service reuse the data between questions on the same design; it changes how the data is written, not what it says).

Agent mode: let Aerion AI do the work

What it is for. One-click jobs that check the design or make small, approved changes.

Where. The AI panel, Agent mode. Both editions. Part of the Aerion AI add-on.

The jobs.

Send outProposal paperworkbothRebuilds the scope letter when it has not been edited, sets full or half labor only when your note asks for it, drafts the customer email, lists what is missing

Steps.

  1. Switch the panel to Agent mode and press a job.
  2. Read each batch of changes it proposes and approve it, unless you turned Ask before each change off.
  3. Undo agent changes puts everything the run changed back.

Rules every job follows. Only the fields the job names, each within its range. Each change is checked first, and a refused one comes back with a plain reason. Nothing is deleted. The design is recalculated and the design checks come back with the result. A Check job cannot change the design even if the AI asks: its tool list is cut down, and any other tool is refused before it runs.

Limits.

  • The Proposal paperwork job always asks before it sets the labor level, even with Ask before each change off, and it never writes over a scope letter you edited.
  • Once Balance room pressure enters a transfer opening, the room counts it as "already in", so the scope letter stops listing that room's opening: draw and scope each one, as the agent's report says.
  • How well a given AI model carries a job out is that model's own. Check the result.

AI help elsewhere in the app

  • Improve the wording on the scope of work letter: the AI rewrites the letter in plain, professional wording from the same facts. It changes no number, size, count or model and adds nothing; the result goes in the box to read first, and Undo puts the letter back (see Reports and letters).
  • Review sheet with AI on the Plan reader and in Plan set to 3D, and Read framing with AI on a structural sheet (see Plans and the plan reader).
  • AI layout review in Best duct layout (see Layout: equipment, registers and ducts).

In each, only what that tool needs (a picture of the sheet, or the route figures and your priorities) goes to your configured AI, and only when you press the button.

Aerion AI on this PC

What it is for. Send Aerion AI's questions to an AI service running on your own computer instead of the online AI service.

Where. Options, card Aerion AI on this PC. In a customer copy the card is there only while the license has the AI add-on.

Steps.

  1. Tick Use the AI on this PC for Aerion AI.
  2. In Model name, type the name of the model on this computer.
  3. Press Test to see what it answers.

Model names with a space. You can type the name the way it is said. Spaces are sent as dashes, and quote marks are left out: "my office model" is sent as my-office-model. If the AI service on this computer still refuses the name, the message says to type the name the way the computer's list of models shows it. In a command window, ollama list shows that list.

What you get. Every Aerion AI question (the panel, the one-click tasks, Improve the wording) goes to the AI service on this computer only (127.0.0.1, port 11434): no key, no internet, nothing leaves the PC, and it does not count against the add-on's monthly allowance. The panel says which AI is answering. In the desktop app the request goes through the app itself, which only ever sends it to this computer.

Limits. The card says it: a model that fits on a desktop is weaker than the online AI service. Use it for wording and explaining, and check any number it gives. The local service must take the same message format Aerion AI uses.

Your own AI key

What it is for. Use your own account with an AI company, billed to you, instead of the add-on's allowance.

Where. Options, card Your own AI key. In a customer copy, with the AI add-on.

Steps.

  1. Pick the AI company: Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI, Google Gemini, xAI (Grok), Mistral, DeepSeek, Groq, OpenRouter, or Other (OpenAI-compatible) with the service's web address.
  2. Paste the API key, name the Model, and press Test.
  3. Remove key puts Aerion AI back as it was.

What you get. Aerion AI's questions go straight from this computer to that company and are billed to that key's account. They do not go through the license server and do not use the add-on's allowance. The panel, the tasks and the agents work the same with any of the companies.

Limits. The key is kept on this computer only, not in the project and not on Aerion's server. How well a given model does the work is that model's own.

Which AI answers. When more than one is set: the AI on this PC first, then your own key, then Aerion's AI service.
