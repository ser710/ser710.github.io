Business, clients and team

Contents

Three pages in the Overview group run the business side: Business (design-fee proposals, time and invoices), Clients (the pipeline of clients and leads across every project) and Team (a business account's seats and shared project files). All three are in both editions.

Set up your firm

What it is for. The details printed on every design proposal and invoice.

Where. Business, card Your firm. Both editions.

Steps. Type the Firm name, Address, Phone, Email and License #, your Hourly rate, Default fee per ft², Payment terms, days, Invoice prefix and Next invoice #.

What you get. They are used on every proposal and invoice, and kept in this browser.

Write a design-fee proposal

What it is for. A proposal to the client for your design work (not the installation; the installation proposal is on the Reports page).

Where. Business, card Design proposal. Both editions.

Steps.

  1. Pick the Fee: Per square foot, Lump sum or Hourly estimate, and type the Rate.
  2. Check the Area (blank = from the rooms).
  3. Set Revisions included, Site visits and Per visit, and how many days the proposal is Good for.
  4. Tick the Scope items: the Manual J room-by-room load calculation, Manual S equipment selection with AHRI match, Manual D duct design, duct layout drawings, plan set and equipment schedules, permit report, fabrication isometrics and cut lists, and the worst-case orientation for builder plans.
  5. Set the Payment milestones (for example a deposit on acceptance and the rest on delivery); Add a milestone adds one.
  6. Type Notes on the proposal.
  7. Press Preview proposal, then Proposal (PDF).

What you get. The fee, worked from your choices, and the proposal PDF.

Log your time

Where. Business, card Time. Both editions.

Steps. Pick the Task (Loads, Equipment, Layout, Duct design, Drafting, Revisions, Site visit, Coordination, Admin), then Start the timer, or Add time by hand.

What you get. The hours logged on this project, the hours not billed and their value, and your effective rate.

Send an invoice

Where. Business, card Invoices. Both editions.

Steps. Press Bill: on a payment milestone from the proposal, or bill the unbilled hours.

What you get. An invoice, numbered from your prefix and next number, due by your payment terms.

Track clients and leads

What it is for. The pipeline across every project: stages, follow-ups, win rate and where the work comes from.

Where. The Clients page. Both editions.

Steps.

  1. Press New client or New job, or Add this project as a job.
  2. Set each job's stage: New lead, Contacted, Proposal sent, On hold, Won or Lost. Set a source on each job to see which sources pay.
  3. Use Search and the stage filter to find jobs.
  4. Export CSV backs the list up or moves it; Import CSV brings one in.

What you get. The open pipeline, its value weighted by stage, the work won this year, the win rate and the average job won; the pipeline by stage; the client list; and Where the work comes from.

Limits. The list is kept in this browser, across every project. Export it to back it up.

Share project files with your team

What it is for. A business buys one subscription with several seats. The owner sets up the business account and invites each person; everyone on it shares project files.

Where. The Team page, and Options, License. Both editions.

Steps.

  1. Activate your license first in Options, License. Until then the Team page says so.
  2. To join a team: the account owner sends you an invite code (TEAM-…). Enter your email and that code in Options, License.

What you get. The page holds the business account: its seats, the invites and the project files everyone shares.

Limits. The Team page needs an activated license.
