Field and service

Contents

Two pages follow the job after the design: Field (install progress, questions from the field and photos) and Start-up & service (the equipment register, start-up readings and the maintenance history). Both are in both editions. The field tests for the energy code are on the Reports page (see Reports and letters).

Track the install

What it is for. Mark what is installed, room by room and duct by duct, and print a punch list.

Where. The Field page, card Progress, and the Field tool on the Layout (Notes group). Both editions.

Steps.

  1. Press Open the plan in field mode, or pick Field on the Layout.
  2. Mark items installed on the plan as the crew finishes them.
  3. Press Show installed on the plan to see what is done (Hide installed on the plan hides it).
  4. Press Preview punch list, Punch list (PDF) or Punch list (CSV).

What you get. The share installed, the items done out of the total, the open questions and the photos, and a table by system and floor: items in, and feet of duct in.

Limits. Progress and questions save with the project. To share between the office and the field, save the project file and open it on the other device. Photos stay on the device that took them.

Ask a question from the field (RFIs)

Where. The Field page, card Questions (RFIs), and the Field tool on the Layout. Both editions.

Steps. On the plan, pick the Field tool, pick Ask a question, and tap where it is. Type the question.

What you get. The question pinned on the plan and listed on the Field page.

Pin a photo

Where. The Field page, card Photos, and the Field tool on the Layout. Both editions.

Steps. With the Field tool, pick Pin a photo and tap the spot.

Limits. Photos stay on the device that took them.

Keep the equipment register

What it is for. Record each piece of equipment's brand, model, serial number, install date and warranty.

Where. Start-up & service, card Equipment register. Both editions.

Steps.

  1. For each row (outdoor unit, air handler, heat kit, thermostat, from the design), type the Brand, Model, Serial, Installed date, Registered date and the parts and labor warranty years.
  2. + Other equipment adds a row. Set every install date to today fills the dates.

What you get. The summary at the top of the page: the next service, the next filter change, the last visit and the equipment count. The next service date also shows on the Dashboard of this project on this computer.

Record the start-up

What it is for. Record the start-up readings against the nameplate, and the start-up checklist.

Where. Start-up & service, one card per system (for example "FAU-1 start-up"). Both editions.

Steps.

  1. Fill in the Visit: start-up date, technician and license number.
  2. Electrical: voltage, and the compressor, outdoor fan and blower amps with their nameplate RLA and FLA.
  3. Airflow and refrigerant: return and supply air temperatures, the drop or rise range from the nameplate, measured airflow, total external static, and the charge check (subcooling, superheat or weigh-in, with its target and measured value).
  4. Tick the Checklist items as they are done (condensate trap and drain, overflow switch, filter, thermostat setup, line insulation, locking refrigerant caps, breaker and disconnect, service clearances, registers and balancing, duct connections, the owner shown the controls, warranty registration). Add Notes.
  5. Press Preview start-up report or Start-up report (PDF).

What you get. A table of each reading, its target, the measured value and a status. Airflow, static and charge are the same readings as the field tests on the Reports page.

Limits. Some targets are the nameplate's, which you enter; the card labels its typical ranges as typical. The equipment maker's installation instructions govern.

Log maintenance

What it is for. Keep the service history and the next due dates.

Where. Start-up & service, card Maintenance. Both editions.

Steps.

  1. Set Service every and Filter every (months).
  2. Log each visit: + Maintenance visit, + Filter change or + Repair, with the date, system, technician and the work done or readings.
  3. Press Preview service record or Service record (PDF).

What you get. The next service and filter dates. They start from an install date, a start-up date or a visit.
