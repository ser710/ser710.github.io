Aerion manual

This manual covers the residential and the commercial editions of Aerion HVAC Design Studio, version 1.5.64. It describes what the app does today, by the labels you see on screen. Where the two editions differ, the section says so.

Aerion does the arithmetic. The designer of record checks the design, the loads, the equipment and the ducts before anything is built, priced or sent for a permit. ACCA approval of Aerion is in progress; it is not an ACCA-approved program yet.

How to read it

  • Page names (Layout, Plans & markup, Plan reader, Load calculator, Options) are the names on the app's page list.
  • Button and field names are written in bold as they appear, for example Tidy labels or Model name.
  • Each task says what it is for, where to find it, the steps, what you get, and its limits.
  • A value the app uses that has no published source is called a default. Replace it with the job's own figure.
  • Screens change. If a label in the app does not match this manual, the app is right and the manual is out of date.

What changed in this manual

  • Version 1.5.64: the manual is rewritten to cover the whole app in both editions, every page and tool, with new pages for plans, design conditions, the Layout (three pages), the home survey, the shop, controls and schedules, codes, field and service, business, BIM models, options and the methods reference. It covers the features up to this version, among them room labels that move, takeoffs kept off trunk reducers, trunk end caps, moving with the keyboard, room pressure with ENERGY STAR targets and plenum returns, duct labels and Tidy selected labels, the residential sizing and code checks, the plan reader's floors, the desktop window guards, Manual S at design conditions with the heat pump balance point, the IMC, IBC and Florida code checks, the commercial outdoor air fixes, Rotate, the update notes, the price book data, building framing, alignment guides, fitting previews, route comparisons and Job review.
  • Version 1.5.57: pages for the features of 1.5.51 to 1.5.57.
