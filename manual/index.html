Aerion manual

This manual covers the residential and the commercial editions of Aerion. It describes what the app does today, by the labels you see on screen. Where the two editions differ, the section says so.

Aerion does the arithmetic. The designer of record checks the design, the loads, the equipment and the ducts before anything is built, priced or sent for a permit.

What changed in this manual

  • Version 1.5.57: pages written for the features of 1.5.51 to 1.5.57 (Snip, Rotate, plan reader duct sizes and several floors on one sheet, plenum returns in the commercial airflow simulation and room pressure, duct labels along and inside their duct, Tidy labels, the model name for the AI on this PC).
  • Pages added for equipment, ducts, price book and estimate, reports and letters, license and updates.

How to read it

  • Page names (Layout, Plans & markup, Plan reader, Load calculator, Options) are the names on the app's page list.
  • Button and field names are written as they appear, for example Tidy labels or Model name.
  • Screens change. If a label in the app does not match this manual, the app is right and the manual is out of date.
