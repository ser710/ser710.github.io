Price book and estimate

Contents

Find a price in the price book

What it is for. Look up store prices for equipment, duct, fittings, air distribution, filters and controls.

Where. The Price book page. Both editions.

Steps.

  1. Type in Search.
  2. Narrow it with Category, Prices (All lines, Store prices, Earlier online lists, Estimated sizes) and Store.
  3. Use Previous and Next to page through.

What you get. The matching lines with their prices. The card says when each store's prices were read. A size no store lists by name is estimated from the other prices and marked est.

Limits. These are retail web prices on the date shown, not your supplier's prices. Load your supplier's sheet on the Materials page for your own pricing.

Use the prices. Use these prices on Materials opens the Materials page and puts web prices on the takeoff. The message says how many prices were loaded and that they are retail prices.

The bill of materials

What it is for. List every part in the design, from the outdoor unit to the hanger strap.

Where. The Materials page, the Bill of materials card. Both editions.

What you get. The parts in groups (Equipment, Air distribution, Duct and fittings, Refrigerant piping on residential, Controls and zoning, Electrical, Install and accessories). Change the design and the list follows. Set Waste on duct, pipe and wire, Refrigerant and Condensate pipe per unit. Preview takeoff shows the takeoff; Takeoff (PDF) and Takeoff (.csv) save it.

Build the estimate

Where. The Materials page: tick Show prices and labor (estimate). Both editions.

Steps.

  1. Tick Show prices and labor (estimate).
  2. Set Markup, Sales tax, Labor rate, Overhead and Profit.
  3. Pick Labor: Full labor (your crew does all of it) or Half labor (the customer's crew does part).
  4. Put prices on the lines: Import price sheet (the CSV or Excel your supply house sends), or Load web retail starter prices.
  5. Add other costs under Job costs with Add job cost.
  6. Press Save prices and labor for all projects to keep your prices and labor hours.

What you get. The Estimate: materials, labor (with hours), overhead, profit and the job price, and how many items are unpriced. Good / Better / Best prices three options; Send prices to proposal puts them on the proposal.

Limits. The labor hours start as example hours. Replace them with your own in the Labor column. The overhead is a share of direct cost, profit a share of cost plus overhead, and sales tax applies to materials only.

Ask suppliers for quotes

Where. The Materials page, the Supplier quotes card. Both editions.

Steps. Type the Supplier and Contact, press Add supplier (with no name, the message asks for the supplier's name first), and set Quotes needed by. The card says the rest: send each supplier its request for quote, type or import the prices that come back, then use the lowest prices in the estimate.
