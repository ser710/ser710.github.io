Aerion in the web browser

Contents

Aerion also runs in a web browser, on a phone, a tablet or any computer, with the same license you bought. Use it on the job site to open a job, check the rooms and the loads, look at the equipment and the estimate, and pull up a report. Both editions are there.

Open it

  • Residential: https://aerionhvacdesign.com/app/
  • Commercial: https://aerionhvacdesign.com/app/commercial.html

The first time, it needs a connection to load. After that it opens with no connection too, and it picks up the newest version by itself whenever it opens online. There is nothing to update by hand.

Activate your license

Where. Options, card License, the same as the desktop app (see License and updates).

Your license covers one PC plus one phone or tablet, at no extra cost. A license key works on one computer at a time, so if Aerion is already on your PC, link the phone or tablet from the PC instead of typing the key (see Link with my PC below). Typing the key in the browser works only when the key is not active on another computer.

Steps, with no PC. Type the Purchase email and the License key and press Activate, or paste an AER1 key.

One browser is one device. The web app counts each browser on each device on its own, with its own machine code on the License card. Before you clear this site's data in the browser, or stop using that browser, use Move to another computer first.

Until a license is active, design work is open and saving, printing and exports are locked, as in the desktop app.

Put it on your home screen

Installed, Aerion opens from its own icon, full screen, like any other app.

  • iPhone and iPad (Safari). Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android (Chrome). Open the browser menu and tap Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  • A computer (Chrome or Edge). Press the install icon at the right of the address bar, or Options, card This browser, Install.

Where your projects are kept

In this browser, on this device. Projects you make or open in the web app are kept in that browser's own storage. They are not sent anywhere unless you turn on Link with my PC (below), which keeps the same jobs on your PC and in this browser. The Projects page and Options, card This browser, say so too.

  • Keep a copy. Save (at the top) saves the project as a project file (.json), which the browser puts in Downloads (on a phone, in Files or Downloads). Do this before you clear the browser's data, and to move a job between devices.
  • Bring a job in. Open (at the top) or Import file on the Projects page opens a project file saved by the web app or the desktop app.
  • Clearing the browser's data removes them. So does uninstalling the browser. On iPhone and iPad, Safari can clear a website's storage after weeks without a visit; a copy on the home screen is kept. Aerion asks the browser to keep its storage, and Options says when the browser has agreed.
  • Room. A browser keeps about 5 MB per site. The Projects page shows how much is used. Save large jobs with drawings under them as files.

Every other file (PDF reports, the plan set, CSV and DXF exports) is a download too.

Link with my PC

Make or change a job on the PC and it shows up in Aerion on your phone or tablet at the job site; change it there and the change comes back to the PC. There is one switch, and nothing else to set up.

1. Link the phone or tablet to your PC's license (once).

  • On the PC: Options, card Link with my phone or tablet (also on the Projects page), press Link a phone or tablet. A code (like ABCD-EF23) and a QR code show. The code works once, for 10 minutes.
  • On the phone or tablet: scan the QR code with the camera. It opens Aerion in the browser and links it by itself. Or open https://aerionhvacdesign.com/app/ (commercial: /app/commercial.html), go to Options, card Link with my PC, type the code under Link to my PC and press Link.
  • The phone now uses your PC's license (its own key for that device; your license key is never shown to it). Linking another phone or tablet later replaces the first. Unlink the phone or tablet on the PC stops it at its next check-in.

2. Turn on the switch. On the PC, Projects, card Link with my phone or tablet: tick Keep my jobs on my PC and my phone in step. The first time, this makes your linked jobs (kept online with your Aerion license) and puts each of your projects there. On the phone the switch turns on by itself when you link it; the PC's jobs show up in Projects.

What goes back and forth. Every project in that edition (residential or commercial), with its rooms, loads, equipment, layout, prices and notes. A change is sent a few seconds after you stop typing. Jobs from the other device show up by themselves: when Aerion opens, when you come back to it, and every minute and a half while it is open. The examples Aerion opens the first time are not sent unless you change them.

What it says. The card and the top bar (next to "Saved locally") say Linked — up to date, Sending… or Offline — will send when online.

No signal at the job site. Keep working. Changes are kept on the phone, marked Offline — will send when online, and go out by themselves when the phone is back online. Closing Aerion in between is fine.

Changed on both. If the same job was changed on the PC and on the phone before either was sent, both are kept: the newer one stays under its name and the other is saved as "*name* (copy from phone)" or "*name* (copy from PC)", on both. Nothing is overwritten. Look at the two and delete the one you do not need.

Big drawings. A linked job can be up to 20 MB. A job over that, usually because of large drawing backgrounds, is sent without its background images, and the card says so; the phone gets the job without them, and the PC keeps its own. A job still over 20 MB without them is not sent, and the card says so.

Deleting. Deleting a linked job deletes it from your linked jobs. The other device keeps its copy, no longer linked, and the card says it was deleted on your other device.

Turning it off. Untick the switch. Jobs stay where they are; nothing is deleted.

The Linked jobs page. Your linked jobs are also listed on the Linked jobs page (the Team tab, which is called Linked jobs while the account is just you): open one, see its versions, or delete it there. To share jobs with other people later, press Invite someone on that page; once someone is invited, it becomes a team page again (see Business, clients and team).

On a phone

The field pages fit a phone screen: Projects, Design conditions, Load calculator (tap a room and its editor comes up), Equipment, Duct sizing, Materials and the estimate, Price book and Reports, and in the commercial edition Commercial loads. Wide tables scroll sideways inside their own box. The page list and the buttons at the top scroll sideways too.

The Layout and the 3D view open on a phone: drag with one finger to pan (use Pan on the toolbar, or turn the 3D view), and pinch with two fingers to zoom. Drawing a whole job is easier on a bigger screen.

What needs the desktop app

DWG drawings open in the browser: Open file at the top, or Import DWG on the Layout, reads them inside the page (AutoCAD R13 to today), the same as in the desktop app. See Files from and to other programs.

These need the Aerion desktop app for Windows:

  • Aerion AI on this PC (a local AI). In the web app, Aerion AI uses your own AI key or the Aerion AI add-on, the same as in the desktop app.
  • Sending a design straight to the BIM add-in. In the browser the job file is saved as a download.
  • Updates by installer. The web app needs none: it is always the newest version.

Limits

  • Without Link with my PC, the web app and the desktop app keep their projects apart. Move a job between them with Save and Open, or turn the link on.
  • Linking needs the internet when jobs are sent and brought in, and a license that is active. A phone browser keeps about 5 MB of jobs; a job too big for it stays on the PC and the card says so.
  • A phone is for checking and small changes in the field. Laying out a whole house or building is easier on a computer.
