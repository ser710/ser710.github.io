Options and tables

Contents

The Options page holds the settings that apply to your work in general: the company and title block, how ducts are drawn, what prints next to registers, the display, the load calculation shortcuts, your defaults, the keyboard keys and more. The Tables page holds the climate list and the fitting and material tables. Both pages are in both editions. The license, updates, code data and AI cards on Options are in License and updates, Code check, energy code and Manual J8 and Aerion AI.

Company and title block

Where. Options, card Company and title block. Both editions.

Steps. Type the Designer, Company, Address line 1 and 2, Phone and Email. Pick the Plan sheet size (E1 42 × 30 in, D 36 × 24 in, C 24 × 18 in, B 17 × 11 in) and the Plan scale.

What you get. They show on the plan set, permit report and fab iso title blocks, and the scope of work letter is written from them.

Duct drawing

Where. Options, card Duct drawing. Both editions.

What it sets. For ducts you draw from now on, on the plan and in 3D: Duct type (Auto (trunks rect, branches round), Rectangular, Hard round, Square, Flat oval, Oval, Triangle, Trapezoid, Parallelogram, Rhombus, Flex), Size (blank = by airflow), Elevation reference, Elevation above floor (blank = auto), Angle snap (Free, 90°, 45°, 30°, 15°), Branch takeoff at tees, Elbows, Snap registers, air handlers and duct ends, Snap onto ducts (makes tee), Snap grid and Match the height of the duct you start from. Change any duct later in its Selection card.

Register annotations

What it is for. Choose what prints next to each register and grille on the plan, the plan set and the DXF.

Where. Options, card Register annotations (commercial: Diffuser annotations). Both editions.

Steps.

  1. Tick, for supply and for return, what to show: Tag (S-1, R-1), Size (W x H), Airflow (cfm), Model / type, Neck / duct size, Face velocity (fpm), Room name.
  2. Pick the Layout (Stacked lines or One line, separated ·) and the Position (below, right of or above the register).
  3. Tick Show the suggested size when none is set; a suggested size is then marked with an asterisk.

Limits. Face velocity uses 70% free area, as the card says. A tag typed on a register overrides the automatic numbering.

Drawing and display

Where. Options, card Drawing and display. Both editions.

What it sets. Canvas style, Duct drawing (Double line, to scale, or Single line), Color ducts by, Color rooms by, Print duct colors on plan set, the drawing aids (Ortho (90° / 45° drawing), Snap to CAD, walls and ducts, Moving a room carries its registers and notes) and the Snap grid (show, spacing X and Y in inches, a major line every so many).

Load calculation shortcuts

Where. Options, card Load calculation. Both editions.

What it sets. Strict Manual J 8th Edition mode (the full settings are on the Manual J8 page), the Glass solar method, People, sensible and People, latent (BTU/h), the Default glass shade factor (no blinds set) and the Heating supply temperature rise. Design temperatures and constructions are on Design conditions.

My defaults

What it is for. Start every new project with your own settings.

Where. Options, card My defaults. Both editions.

Steps. Set up a project the way you like, then press Save these as my defaults. Apply my defaults to this project puts them on the open project. Clear my defaults removes them. Export settings and Import settings move them to another computer.

What you get. Saved: the company and title block, duct design, fabrication, drawing and load settings, and the fitting equivalent lengths. Not saved: rooms, ducts and design conditions.

About. The card shows the app's name and the load engine, and that project data is saved in the browser: use Save in the top bar to keep a file.

Keyboard shortcuts

What it is for. Change the keys for the Layout tools.

Where. Options, card Keyboard shortcuts. Both editions.

Steps.

  1. Click a tool's key, then press the key you want: a letter or a number, alone or with Shift.
  2. Backspace or Delete while choosing leaves the tool with no key. Esc cancels.
  3. Reset on a row puts that tool's own key back; Reset all keys puts every key back.

What you get. A key another tool has moves to the tool you give it to, and the other tool is left with no key (a message says so). The keys are kept on this computer, not in the project. A tool's button shows its key in its tip. The default keys are listed in Layout: drawing the plan.

Limits. Not changeable: Delete, Esc, Enter, Space, the arrows (they move the selection, or the plan when nothing is selected; Ctrl+arrow moves 1/4 in.), [ and ] (rotate), + and - (zoom), and the Ctrl shortcuts. While a duct run is being drawn, numbers are its typed length. Two-key shortcuts (AL for Align, CO for Copy) keep working.

Wall openings and floor penetrations

Where. Options, card Wall openings and floor penetrations. Both editions.

Steps. Type the Clearance around the duct (blank = 1) in inches each side.

Limits. The 1 in. default is to replace with what the framing and any fire-stopping detail call for. See Layout: equipment, registers and ducts.

Building framing

Where. Options, card Building framing. Both editions. See Layout: 3D, sections, airflow and framing.

The climate table

What it is for. The list of design locations the Design conditions page picks from.

Where. The Tables page, card Climate table. Both editions.

What you get. For each location: elevation, the summer 1% dry bulb and its coincident wet bulb, the winter 99% dry bulb, the moisture difference in grains at 55%, 50% and 45% indoor RH (at 75°F indoor), the daily range class and a source note. A negative grains value means the outdoor air is drier than indoors, and the load treats it as zero. Pick a State to narrow the list. Use sets a location on the project.

Steps to add your own.

  1. Add location, or Save current design conditions to keep the project's conditions as a location.
  2. Or paste rows in Paste locations straight from Manual J Table 1A or another program's weather list (location, elevation, 99% heating, 1% cooling, coincident wet bulb, grains at 55, 50 and 45% RH, daily range). Spaces, tabs or | between columns all work. Type the State code for rows without one and press Add pasted rows.
  3. Reset to built-in table puts the list back.

Limits. The table's values are starter values. Confirm them against the jurisdiction's Manual J Table 1A (commercial: the ASHRAE climatic design conditions for the site).
