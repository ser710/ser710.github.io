Pick the equipment size for a system

What it is for. Compare equipment sizes with the system's load and pick one.

Where. The Equipment page, the Equipment inputs and Equipment selection cards. Both editions.

Steps.

  1. In Equipment inputs, pick the System (each air handler or unit, or Whole house).
  2. Pick the system type in the second System list (for example Heat pump (strip backup) or AC + gas furnace; the commercial edition has no gas furnace choice here).
  3. Leave Nominal size on Automatic, or pick a size.
  4. Set Airflow per ton and, if you need it, Airflow override (0 = auto).

What you get. Equipment selection shows the total load, the size selected, the system airflow and the heating load, with a table of each nominal size: total BTU/h, % of load, sensible, latent and a verdict. On the ranch sample, picking 3 ton changes Selected to 3 ton and System airflow to 1,200 cfm.

Limits.

  • The nominal sizes are nominal capacities, not the maker's data at your design conditions. Use the model's rated or expanded data for the final pick.
  • The equipment checks are being changed; this section will be updated when that work ships.
  • The designer of record checks the selection against ACCA Manual S.

Load a certified model

What it is for. Put a certified outdoor unit and indoor unit match on the system.

Where. The Equipment page, Equipment inputs, the Brand, Type, Series and Size lists and the Use model … buttons. Both editions.

Steps.

  1. Pick Brand, Type, Series and Size.
  2. Press Use model … (the button names the model). The model number goes into the model fields.

Other ways.

  • ENERGY STAR heat pump directory: set Tons, SEER2 ≥ and Cold climate, pick a type and press Search, then load a match.
  • Read certificate and Use these rated points read an AHRI certificate.
  • Equipment database: Import AHRI matches and Import expanded performance take an AHRI Directory export or a maker's table as CSV or Excel. They stay in this browser for every project.

Air filter pressure drop

What it is for. Find the pressure drop of an air filter at your airflow and size.

Where. The Equipment page, the Air filter database card. Both editions.

Steps.

  1. In Airflow from, pick the system, or Typed airflow and type the Airflow in cfm.
  2. Pick Size, How many, and Design for (Clean (initial), Mid-life or At change-out).
  3. Filter the list by Type, MERV, Show and Sort by, or Search.
  4. Press Curve on up to eight filters to chart them in Pressure drop vs airflow.

What you get. Each filter's drop, read from that filter's own published data. † marks a value outside the published data, extrapolated. The Sources and method card says where the data comes from.

Add your own filter. In Add a filter, type the points from the maker's sheet and press Add the filter. It is saved with this project.

Commercial and hydronic equipment (commercial)

Where. The Equipment page, the Commercial and hydronic equipment card. Commercial edition only.

What it is for. Pick a packaged rooftop unit, VRF outdoor unit, air handler (CHW coil), air-cooled or water-cooled chiller, or condensing boiler from a generic catalog, sized against the system's load.

Steps. Press the type, set Min capacity and Max capacity (× load), then Select.

Limits. The card says it: the catalog is generic, at standard rating conditions. Confirm the final pick with the maker's data. Rooftop unit sizing is being changed; this section will be updated when that work ships. Open Commercial loads on Equipment inputs goes to the Commercial loads page.
