Home survey (existing homes)

Contents

The Home survey page is for replacements and retrofits with no plans. Walk the house room by room on a phone or tablet, or bring in a LiDAR room scan. The rooms go straight into the load calculation. Residential edition only.

Walk the house room by room

What it is for. Build the load calculation from a site visit.

Where. Home survey, cards Existing-home survey, Add a room and Surveyed rooms. Residential edition.

Steps.

  1. In Existing-home survey, set Front of the house faces and the Year built. Sides are named as you face the front door from the street. The front facing stays in step with Design conditions and the Layout.
  2. Leave Draw surveyed rooms as boxes on the Layout ticked to see the rooms on the plan, or clear it.
  3. In Add a room, type the Name, pick the Type, and type Length, ft (front-back sides), Width, ft and Ceiling, ft.
  4. Pick the Floor (Slab, Over crawl space, Over garage, Over an unheated basement, Basement (below grade), Over outdoors, Over a conditioned floor) and what is Above (Attic, Roof (cathedral), A conditioned floor).
  5. In the wall table, tick each outside wall (Front, Back and the sides), its Length ft, its windows (quantity x size) and doors. Leave a side unchecked when it is an inside wall. A blank length is the room's length (front and back) or width (sides).
  6. Press Add room. Clear empties the form.

What you get. The room in Surveyed rooms, with the count and floor area, and in the load calculation.

Limits. Window size code 3050 is 3'0" wide x 5'0" tall; 36x60 in inches also works.

Describe the house

What it is for. Set the whole house's windows, insulation, walls, air tightness and ducts from what you see on site.

Where. Home survey, card The house. Residential edition.

Steps.

  1. Pick Windows (or keep the project default), Attic insulation with its Depth, in., and Walls (or Keep the project wall).
  2. Pick Air tightness: Not tested: use the project value, Tight (newer, sealed): about 5 ACH50, Average: about 10 ACH50 or Leaky (older, unsealed): about 15 ACH50.
  3. Pick where Ducts are in the house and the Duct condition: Use the project value, Sealed with mastic, in good shape: 4%, Average, some leaks: 8% or Leaky, disconnected or crushed runs: 15%.
  4. Add Notes and press Apply to the project.

What you get. The choices are written into the project's design conditions.

Limits. The card says it: these are typical values, not test results. Enter a blower door or duct test in the project when you have one.

Record the existing equipment

What it is for. Note what is there now, for the proposal and for comparing the replacement size.

Where. Home survey, card Existing equipment, Add a unit. Residential edition.

Print the existing conditions

Where. Home survey, under Surveyed rooms: Preview existing conditions and Existing conditions (PDF). Residential edition.

Import a LiDAR room scan

What it is for. Bring in rooms scanned with a phone or tablet.

Where. Home survey, card Room scan import. Residential edition.

Steps.

  1. Pick the scan file: a JSON file in Apple's RoomPlan format (CapturedRoom or CapturedStructure).
  2. Set Plan north (Up, Up-right, Right and so on).
  3. For rooms from separate scans, pick which walls are outside walls: Walls with windows, All walls, or None (mark them later).

What you get. Rooms scanned together come in placed; their outside walls are the ones with no room behind them. Rooms from separate scans are lined up side by side.

Limits. Check each room's outside walls, windows and doors after the import.
