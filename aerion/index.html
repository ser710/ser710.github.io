From floor plan to shop drawings in one program.

Aerion HVAC Design Studio does room-by-room heating and cooling loads, equipment selection, duct layout and sizing, code checks and fabrication-ready shop packages, all from the same drawing. Built by a residential HVAC designer with nine years of load calculation and system design behind it.

Everything a design job needs

Draw the house once. Loads, airflow, duct sizes, equipment and the paperwork all follow from that drawing and update when it changes.

Room-by-room loads

Sensible, latent and heating loads from the walls, glass, ceilings and floors you draw, with outdoor design conditions and climate zones built in for every US county.

Equipment selection

Match heat pumps, furnaces and air handlers to the load, with expanded performance at your design conditions.

Duct layout and sizing

Draw trunks, branches and flex, or let Aerion lay them out. Sizes come from friction rate and available static pressure, room by room.

Plans and markup

Bring in PDF, DXF and DWG plans, scale them, read the rooms off the sheet and mark up drawings, with no separate program.

Code checks

Energy code and mechanical code checks for the West Coast, the Southwest and Texas, with the jurisdiction's adopted edition.

3D and BIM export

See the building and ductwork in 3D, and send the full duct design to your BIM modeling program as connected 3D pieces.

Shop package

A bound PDF of fittings, cut lists and diagrams for the sheet metal shop, plus DXF cut files for plasma tables on commercial jobs.

Reports and pricing

Permit-ready reports, a bill of materials and a built-in material price book so bids come out of the same job file.

Loads you can check

Every room shows its full breakdown: walls, glass conduction and solar, ceiling, floor, infiltration and ducts. Change a window and the loads, airflow and duct sizes all follow.

Duct sizing that shows its work

Friction rate, total effective length and available static pressure for every run, with round and rectangular sizes and velocities you can hand to an inspector.

Questions

What do I need to run Aerion?

A Windows 10 or 11 computer (64-bit) with an internet connection now and then for license renewal. Design work runs offline.

Can I try it before paying?

Yes. Every plan has a 14-day free trial. Until a license is active, design work stays open; saving, printing and exports unlock with the license.

How many computers can I use?

Each license runs on one computer at a time. Use Move to another computer in Options › License to switch. Businesses can add seats for each designer.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time from your order page. Your license keeps working to the end of the period you paid for. See the refund policy.

Where does the data come from?

Outdoor design conditions, climate zones, degree days and code adoption records are built in from published government and industry sources. Licensed tables that cannot be bundled can be loaded from your own copy.

How do I get help?

Reply to your purchase or receipt email and it comes straight to us. You can also reach us from your order page.
